# Setting Up Your Organisation

Organisation Setup creates the reporting structure used throughout workSPOC Voice.

## Why this matters

A well-designed structure lets teams analyse survey responses across meaningful dimensions and compare like with like. It also prevents inconsistent labels from fragmenting reports.

## Before you begin

Gather the current organisation hierarchy, reporting definitions, operating year and a named owner for future changes.

## How it works

Configure only the dimensions relevant to your programme:

| Structural element | Typical use |
|---|---|
| Operating Year | Align feedback cycles to the reporting period |
| Division | Separate major lines of business |
| Business Unit | Compare accountable operating teams |
| Region | Analyse geographical performance |
| Location / Site | Identify site-level variation |
| Department | Route findings to internal owners |
| Function | Compare cross-business capabilities |
| Offering | Understand products or services |

## Step-by-step

1. Open **Control → Organisation Setup**.
2. Set the active Operating Year.
3. Add top-level Divisions and Business Units.
4. Add Regions and Locations where geographic analysis is required.
5. Add Departments, Functions and Offerings used in reporting.
6. Review naming, parent relationships and active status.
7. Save the structure and test it with a sample contact or survey.

## Example

Northstar Services configures two Divisions, three Regions and five Offerings. Its quarterly dashboard can then compare onboarding feedback across the APAC and EMEA regions while filtering to the Managed Support offering.

## Best practices

- Mirror reporting needs, not every detail in the HR system.
- Use names people recognise and avoid unexplained abbreviations.
- Nominate one structure owner and review inactive values quarterly.
- Avoid changing labels during a live measurement cycle.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do we need every structural element?** No. Use only dimensions that support ownership, targeting or analysis.

**Can structure change later?** Yes, but assess the effect on trends and historic reporting first.
