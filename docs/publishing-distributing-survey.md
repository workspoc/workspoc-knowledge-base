# Publishing and Distributing a Survey

Complete final quality checks, publish the survey and deliver invitations to the intended audience.

## Why this matters

Publication creates a live measurement event. Careful checks protect customer trust, delivery quality and the integrity of comparable responses.

## Before you begin

Confirm approval, dates, audience size, sender details, invitation copy, privacy wording and an internal test response.

## Step-by-step

1. Open the survey's **Review & publish** area.
2. Resolve warnings for missing questions, respondents or required settings.
3. Preview the complete respondent experience.
4. Send a test invitation and complete it on desktop and mobile.
5. Confirm the final respondent count and remove exclusions.
6. Select **Publish** and verify the live status.
7. Start distribution using the configured invitation channel.
8. Review delivery status shortly after launch.

## Example

A relationship survey is approved for 450 contacts. The owner tests links and translations, removes 12 opted-out contacts and publishes at 09:00 in the audience's primary time zone.

## Best practices

- Use a recognisable sender and concise subject line.
- Avoid overlapping invitations to the same audience.
- Keep the survey window long enough for the audience context.
- Record the approved version and launch decision.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why can’t I publish?** The survey may have no eligible respondents, no questions, invalid dates or another required setting missing.

**Can I add respondents later?** Availability depends on survey status and governance. Validate the complete audience before publication where possible.
