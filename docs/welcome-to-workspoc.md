# Welcome to workSPOC

workSPOC Voice is an enterprise Voice of Customer platform that helps teams turn customer feedback into coordinated, measurable improvement.

## Why this matters

Customer evidence is most useful when it is connected to the business, analysed consistently and owned through to action. workSPOC brings configuration, surveys, insight and improvement into one operating rhythm.

## How it works

Teams configure their organisation and measurement framework, prepare customers and questions, run surveys, interpret results and create owned actions. Each stage builds on the one before it.

:::tip A good first visit
Start with the [Quick Start Guide](./quick-start-guide.md), then use the checklist to confirm your setup.
:::

## What you can do

- Model the organisation and customer landscape.
- Maintain a reusable VOC framework and question library.
- Design and distribute targeted surveys.
- Compare responses and identify themes.
- Assign actions and track measurable improvement.

## Example

A regional service team surveys key accounts after delivery. Results are segmented by region and offering, revealing that proactive communication is weakest in one business unit. The team assigns an action owner, improves the contact cadence and measures the next survey cycle.

## Best practices

- Agree measurement outcomes before configuring the platform.
- Use clear naming conventions across organisational structures.
- Keep permissions and contacts current.
- Close the loop by communicating actions to stakeholders.
