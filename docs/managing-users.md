# Managing Users

Add colleagues to workSPOC Voice, provide appropriate access and keep permissions current.

## Why this matters

Good access management protects customer information and ensures surveys, insights and actions are handled by accountable people.

## Before you begin

Confirm the person's business email, responsibilities, organisational scope and required role. Only authorised administrators should manage users.

## Step-by-step

1. Open **Control → Manage Users** and select **Add user**.
2. Enter the user's name and verified work email.
3. Select the role that most closely matches their responsibilities.
4. Limit organisational scope where appropriate.
5. Review permissions, then send the invitation.
6. Periodically review last activity and access.
7. Disable a user promptly when access is no longer required.

## Example

A survey manager needs to build and monitor surveys but does not administer organisation settings. Assign the survey-management role and relevant business-unit scope instead of full administrative access.

## Best practices

- Apply least-privilege access.
- Do not share user accounts.
- Review privileged users and leavers regularly.
- Disable rather than repurpose former user records.

## Frequently asked questions

**Who can create or manage users?** Users with the appropriate administrative permission.

**What happens when a user is disabled?** They can no longer access the platform; their historic work remains available for audit and continuity.
