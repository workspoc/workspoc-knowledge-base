# Understanding Survey Responses

A response is a respondent's submitted evidence for a survey. Interpret it in the context of the question, response scale, survey timing and audience.

Check completion status, sample size and missing data before aggregating. Protect confidentiality and avoid drawing conclusions from identifiable comments or very small groups.
