# Best Practices

- Begin every survey with a clear decision and owner.
- Maintain one governed organisation and measurement vocabulary.
- Reuse approved questions when comparison matters.
- Keep contact information permissioned, current and minimal.
- Test every respondent journey before publication.
- Review representation before interpreting scores.
- Turn priorities into owned, measurable actions.
- Communicate what changed as a result of feedback.
