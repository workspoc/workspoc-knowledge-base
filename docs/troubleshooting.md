# Troubleshooting

Use these checks to resolve common configuration and survey issues before contacting support.

## Common issues

| Problem | Possible cause | Suggested resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Survey cannot be published | No respondents or required configuration is missing | Open the publish checklist, add eligible respondents and resolve every required item |
| Contact not appearing in survey audience | Incorrect classification or incomplete contact information | Review the contact record, audience filters and required fields |
| User cannot access a module | Role or permission does not allow access | Ask an administrator to compare the role with the user's responsibilities |
| Question not appearing in survey | Question filters or template selection may exclude it | Clear filters, check approval status and confirm the template contains the question |
| Survey invitation not received | Incorrect contact information or email delivery issue | Verify the address and delivery status; check suppression or bounce details |

## Before escalating

1. Record the screen, survey or record affected.
2. Note the exact message and when it occurred.
3. Confirm whether other users or records are affected.
4. Check recent changes to permissions, filters or configuration.
5. Avoid repeatedly publishing or re-importing while investigating.

## Frequently asked questions

**Will retrying create duplicates?** Some actions can. Review current status before repeating an import, invitation or creation step.

**What should I include in a support request?** Provide business impact, steps to reproduce, time, affected record and a screenshot without unnecessary personal data.
