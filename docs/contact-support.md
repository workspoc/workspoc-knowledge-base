# Contact Support

Before requesting support, review [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md) and capture the information needed to investigate safely.

Include the business impact, affected page or record, exact message, time, steps to reproduce and whether others are affected. Add a screenshot only when it does not expose unnecessary customer or personal information.

For this internal prototype, use your usual workSPOC team support channel.
