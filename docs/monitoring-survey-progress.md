# Monitoring Survey Progress

Track delivery, participation and completion while a survey is open.

## Why this matters

Monitoring helps teams identify delivery problems early and use proportionate reminders without distorting the respondent experience.

## How it works

Survey status summarises invited, delivered, opened, started and completed responses. Filters can highlight patterns by audience segment or organisational dimension.

## Step-by-step

1. Open the live survey and select **Progress**.
2. Confirm invitations were delivered to the expected audience.
3. Review completion rate and compare it with the target.
4. Investigate bounced or invalid contact details.
5. Check whether specific segments are under-represented.
6. Send a planned reminder to eligible non-respondents.
7. Record any delivery decision and reassess before closing.

## Example

After five days, overall completion is 38%, but partner participation is 18%. The owner checks delivery, confirms no technical issue and sends a targeted reminder with three days remaining.

## Best practices

- Agree response targets before launch.
- Focus on representation, not only the overall percentage.
- Limit reminders and honour opt-outs.
- Do not interpret partial results as final conclusions.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I monitor survey completion?** Open the survey's Progress view to review invitation and response statuses.

**Why do invited and delivered totals differ?** Invalid addresses, suppression rules or delivery failures can reduce the delivered count.
