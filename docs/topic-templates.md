# Topic Templates

Topic Templates are reusable collections of questions that accelerate survey creation and standardise how a topic is measured.

## Why this matters

Templates help distributed teams begin with governed content while still allowing a survey to reflect its audience and purpose.

## Before you begin

Confirm that each included question is approved, current and uses compatible response scales where comparison is expected.

## Step-by-step

1. Open **Surveys → Topic Templates**.
2. Create a template with a specific name and purpose.
3. Search the Question Repository and add approved questions.
4. Arrange questions in a logical respondent journey.
5. Add guidance about when the template should be used.
6. Review and make the template available to survey designers.

## Example

An **Account Onboarding** template contains eight approved questions covering clarity, timeliness, effort and early value. Regional teams reuse it and add at most two local questions.

## Best practices

- Keep templates focused and short.
- Assign an owner and review date.
- Use version notes when material wording changes.
- Treat templates as a strong starting point, not a reason to ask irrelevant questions.
