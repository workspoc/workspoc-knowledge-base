# Planning a Survey

Define the decision, audience and action process before designing questions.

## Planning checklist

1. State the business objective and accountable owner.
2. Define the audience, timing and required representation.
3. Identify the framework areas to measure.
4. Agree privacy, contact and approval requirements.
5. Plan how results will be reviewed and acted upon.
