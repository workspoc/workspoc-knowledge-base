# Creating Your First Survey

Create a focused survey that connects a clear business objective to a relevant audience and actionable insight.

## Why this matters

Effective surveys begin with decisions, not questions. A clear objective keeps the experience concise and makes it easier to act on the results.

## Before you begin

Prepare a survey owner, objective, target audience, open and close dates, approved questions and a plan for reviewing results.

## Step-by-step

1. Open **Survey Management** and select **Create survey**.
2. Add a clear internal name, purpose and accountable owner.
3. Choose a Topic Template or start with approved repository questions.
4. Arrange sections and confirm response formats.
5. Configure the respondent audience and exclusions.
6. Set dates, invitation text and reminder rules.
7. Preview on desktop and mobile, then send an internal test.
8. Resolve validation issues and complete the publish review.

## Example

A Customer Success team creates a 10-question onboarding survey for contacts who completed implementation in the previous 30 days. The survey maps results to Customer Engagement and includes one open-text improvement prompt.

## Best practices

- Ask only questions that support a decision or action.
- Put essential questions early and group related topics.
- State the purpose and expected completion time.
- Avoid leading wording and unnecessary mandatory fields.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can a survey be changed after it is published?** Changes may be restricted to protect response integrity. Review carefully before publication and create a new version when measurement content must change.
