# Quick Start Guide

Use this practical journey to prepare workSPOC Voice and complete your first Voice of Customer cycle.

## Before you begin

Confirm your programme sponsor, primary administrator, target customer group and the business question your first survey should answer.

## Step-by-step

1. **Configure the organisation.** Add the operating year and the structural dimensions you intend to report against.
2. **Add users.** Give programme leads and contributors the minimum access needed for their responsibilities.
3. **Configure the VOC framework.** Agree Pillars, Domains and Practices before linking questions.
4. **Prepare contacts.** Import clean, permissioned contacts and classify them for audience selection.
5. **Build the question library.** Reuse approved wording and group common sets into Topic Templates.
6. **Create and publish the survey.** Check settings, audience, dates and invitation content before launch.
7. **Monitor, analyse and act.** Watch participation, interpret patterns and create actions with clear owners and due dates.

## Best practices

- Keep the first survey focused and short.
- Test with an internal contact before publishing.
- Define how results will be reviewed before invitations go out.
- Record a baseline so improvement can be measured later.
