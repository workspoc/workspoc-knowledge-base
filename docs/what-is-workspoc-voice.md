# What is workSPOC Voice?

workSPOC Voice is an enterprise platform for managing the full Voice of Customer cycle—from programme configuration and survey delivery to insight and improvement action.

It gives teams shared structures, reusable content and a visible route from customer evidence to business ownership. Use the [Platform Overview](./platform-overview.md) to explore each capability.
