# Managing Contacts

Contacts form the audience for workSPOC Voice surveys and connect feedback to relevant customer entities.

## Why this matters

Accurate, well-classified contacts improve invitation delivery, audience selection and meaningful analysis while reducing duplicate or inappropriate outreach.

## How it works

- **Customer entities** represent organisations or accounts.
- **Entity contacts** connect people to an organisation and business relationship.
- **Individual contacts** represent respondents managed independently.
- **Classification** segments contacts as Customer, Distributor, Dealer, Partner, Supplier or Employee.

## Step-by-step

1. Open **Control → Contacts**.
2. Add or import customer entities first where account relationships matter.
3. Add contact names, email details, classification and relevant organisation attributes.
4. Link entity contacts to the correct customer entity.
5. Review import validation messages and resolve duplicates.
6. Create a filtered audience and verify sample records before survey use.

## Example

A partner survey targets senior contacts at active distributor entities in APAC. Classification, region and role data create the audience without manually selecting every respondent.

## Best practices

- Import only permissioned, relevant contact data.
- Validate email addresses and required fields.
- Use stable identifiers to prevent duplicates.
- Refresh ownership and classification before each survey cycle.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I import customer contacts?** Yes. Prepare the file using the expected columns and review validation results before confirming.

**Can different surveys use different customer groups?** Yes. Build audiences using contact attributes and classifications.
