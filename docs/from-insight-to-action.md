# From Insight to Action

Convert validated customer evidence into an owned improvement action with a measurable outcome.

## Why this matters

A VOC programme creates value when the organisation responds. Action management makes the decision, ownership and outcome visible through the next measurement cycle.

## Before you begin

Validate the insight, identify affected customers or processes, involve the team closest to the issue and agree what evidence will show improvement.

## How it works

An action connects a finding to an owner, priority, due date, milestones and success measure. Progress can then be reviewed alongside the insight that prompted it.

## Step-by-step

1. Open the relevant insight and select **Create action**.
2. Write a specific outcome-focused title.
3. Summarise the customer evidence and affected scope.
4. Assign one accountable owner and supporting contributors.
5. Set priority, due date and milestones.
6. Define a success measure and baseline.
7. Review progress regularly and attach evidence.
8. Close only after the outcome is verified.

## Example

**Insight:** Strategic accounts report inconsistent implementation updates. **Action:** Introduce a weekly sponsor update for all strategic implementations within 30 days. **Measure:** Raise Proactive Communication from 61 to 72 in the next cycle.

## Best practices

- Address the cause, not simply the symptom.
- Keep one accountable owner per action.
- Use measures the owner can influence.
- Communicate completed improvements back to customers.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do insights become improvement actions?** Create an action from validated evidence, assign ownership and define a measurable outcome.

**When should an action be closed?** When agreed completion evidence and the success criteria have been reviewed—not merely when tasks are finished.
