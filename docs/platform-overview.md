# Platform Overview

workSPOC Voice connects four working areas: **Control** for foundational data, **Surveys** for listening, **Insights** for interpretation and **Actions** for improvement.

Administrators typically configure the foundation; programme teams prepare content and surveys; business owners review evidence and deliver actions.
