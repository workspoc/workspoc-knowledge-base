# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Action | Owned improvement work linked to customer evidence |
| Contact | A person who may be included in a survey audience |
| Domain | A measurable area within a Pillar |
| Insight | An evidence-based interpretation that supports a decision |
| Pillar | A high-level strategic measurement area |
| Practice | A specific capability or behaviour within a Domain |
| Respondent | A contact invited to or completing a survey |
| Response Theme | A recurring subject identified in feedback |
| Topic Template | A reusable collection of approved questions |
| VOC | Voice of Customer |
