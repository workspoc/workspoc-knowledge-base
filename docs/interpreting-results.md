# Interpreting Results

Interpret results by combining quantitative movement, respondent representation, qualitative context and business knowledge.

## A practical review

1. Validate data quality and sample context.
2. Identify material patterns, not isolated values.
3. Compare relevant segments and prior cycles.
4. Explore comments and themes.
5. Test plausible explanations with responsible teams.
6. Agree the evidence-based priority and next action.
