# Understanding Survey Insights

Use survey insights to move from individual answers to defensible patterns, priorities and decisions.

## Why this matters

Scores alone do not explain what to do. workSPOC Voice connects response evidence with the VOC framework and business dimensions so teams can find meaningful variation and identify improvement opportunities.

## Before you begin

Confirm the survey is sufficiently complete, review the respondent mix and understand the scale, benchmark and filter context.

## How it works

Insights can be explored by survey, question, Pillar, Domain, Practice and relevant organisational or contact dimensions. Quantitative trends and response themes should be considered together.

## Step-by-step

1. Open **Insights → Survey Insights** and select a survey cycle.
2. Check response volume and audience representation.
3. Review headline results and movement from the comparison period.
4. Explore Pillars, then drill into contributing Domains and Practices.
5. Apply one filter at a time and note the comparison baseline.
6. Read qualitative comments and response themes for context.
7. Record evidence, confidence and an accountable next step.

## Example

Overall Customer Experience is stable, but Proactive Communication declines by nine points among strategic accounts in one region. Comments consistently mention delayed status updates, providing a specific improvement hypothesis.

## Best practices

- Check sample size before comparing small segments.
- Distinguish correlation from proven cause.
- Look for consistent evidence across scores, trends and comments.
- Protect respondent confidentiality when sharing results.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why does a dashboard result change when I apply a filter?** The calculation now reflects only responses that match the selected criteria.

**Does the lowest score always become the top priority?** No. Consider strategic importance, trend, reach, confidence and ability to act.
